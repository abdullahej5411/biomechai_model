4. Key Machine Learning Terminology Explained

A. What is Macro Recall?

Macro Recall is the unweighted, plain average of the recall across all 7 exercise classes:
Macro Recall = (Recall_Curl + Recall_Knees + Recall_Jack + Recall_Lunge + Recall_Plank + Recall_Pushup + Recall_Squat) / 7

  • Why it matters: Standard accuracy treats every clip equally. If a dataset has 200 squats and only 10 pushups, a model could predict squat every time and score 90% accuracy while failing pushups entirely.
  • Our Result: Because our Macro Recall is 61.39% (very close to our 63.54% accuracy), it mathematically proves that our model performs reliably across all 7 exercises rather than favoring a dominant class.

B. What does High Confidence Mean?

  • Model Prediction Confidence (e.g. 97.58% on live squats): The Softmax probability output of the neural network. A score of 97.58% means the network is 97.58% certain of the exercise class with near-zero hesitation or confusion.
  • Statistical Confidence Interval (e.g. 95% CI [56.5% – 70.0%]): A mathematical proof that if we tested our model 100 times on 100 new groups of human athletes, 95 times out of 100 the real-world accuracy will fall between 56.5% and 70.0%.

5. What is Inside `run_cloud_server.bat`?

What to Speak if Asked About the Batch File: "The .bat file is our 1-click automated deployment launcher. It executes two coordinated steps: 1. It activates our Python virtual environment and starts the FastAPI ASGI server (Uvicorn) on local Port 8000, loading our PyTorch PoseC3D AI model into memory. 2. After a 3-second delay, it launches our permanent static ngrok cloud tunnel (persevere-kindred-tasty.ngrok-free.dev) mapped directly to port 8000. This allows our mobile app to communicate securely over HTTPS/WSS from any network (cellular 4G/5G or university Wi-Fi) without needing complex router port-forwarding."

6. Panel Q&A Quick Reference (Tough Questions & Direct Answers)

Q: "Why is your accuracy 63.54% and not 95% like some papers claim?"
Answer: "95%+ scores in fitness projects are almost always due to clip-level data leakage, where clips from the same subject appear in both training and testing. In BioMechAI, we enforced strict zero-leakage video-disjoint testing across 115 unseen humans. On this honest benchmark, PoseC3D achieves 63.54% Top-1 Accuracy, 61.39% Macro Recall, and 97.58% confidence on live inference."
Q: "What is the difference between PoseC3D v5 and v7?"
Answer: "PoseC3D v5 was trained on 1,720 clips and achieved 53.38% on the frozen benchmark. PoseC3D v7 is our expanded, cleaned champion: we audited 4,434 clips, eliminated 987 duplicate clips, removed 284 static/low-motion frames, and trained on 3,159 clean clips. On strictly unique test clips, v7 jumped to 63.54% Top-1 Accuracy."
Q: "Can your system work completely offline without the internet?"
Answer: "Yes, sir. If there is no internet, Uvicorn already listens on local Wi-Fi port 8000. Furthermore, the mobile app contains an on-device offline kinematic engine (FormValidationService) that continues rep counting, red skeleton highlighting, and audio safety warnings even if the backend is completely powered off."
